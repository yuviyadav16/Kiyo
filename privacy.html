Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Kiyo AI, we collect minimal data required to provide a personalized educational experience. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, Email ID, Date of Birth, State, and City.
  • Educational Profile: Your selected Class, Stream (PCB/PCM), Target Exam, and Goals.
  • Usage Data: Queries asked to our AI teachers to improve learning outcomes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for educational purposes:

  • To verify your account and maintain security via OTPs.
  • To customize your dashboard and recommend relevant study materials.
  • To provide context to our AI models so they can answer your doubts according to your grade and syllabus.

3. AI and Third-Party Services

Kiyo AI utilizes advanced Artificial Intelligence APIs. While your educational queries are processed by these models to generate answers, we do not share your personally identifiable information (like email or passwords) with third-party AI providers.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including secure databases (Firebase) and encrypted connections, to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or permanently delete your account and associated data at any time through the App Settings menu in your dashboard.
